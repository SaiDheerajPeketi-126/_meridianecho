Contact

Email developer@blackandblue.co.in with Meridian Echo, your app and Android versions and a short description. Do not send passwords, tokens, reviewer codes, payment-card data or private save exports.

Progress and recovery

Checkpoints, choices, optional stories and settings are stored locally, with a recovery save backup. If a checkpoint cannot be saved, free device storage and use the retry action. Starting a new journey or clearing app storage can lose progress. No publisher cloud-save or recovery service is provided.

Purchase access

The opening chapter is free. A configured later campaign uses a one-time Google Play unlock. Use the matching Play account and Restore purchases when needed. An unavailable price, failed status check or cached status is not confirmation of a completed purchase or current entitlement. Google Play and RevenueCat handle purchase and restoration requests independently of optional reporting choices.

Privacy and technical reports

On the title screen or in Journal / Pause, open Privacy choices. Optional Firebase activation remains unavailable until audience and regional consent eligibility are established. Local crash capture may continue until restart after disabling it. Pending report Send and Delete actions are requests, not provider completion receipts.

In a Play Games-enabled build, startup and authentication operate separately from those Firebase choices. Settings shows connection status and an available Sign in action; an unavailable connection does not block gameplay. Manage your games account, sign-in preferences and profile visibility through Google Play Games settings.

For details see privacy and retention and deletion choices.
